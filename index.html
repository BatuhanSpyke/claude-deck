Keyboard shortcuts
Drag a pane by its title bar to move it: drop on an edge to split there, in the middle to swap, on a tab to move it to that tab, or on an empty part of the tab bar to give it its own tab. Drag tabs to reorder them. Press esc to cancel any drag.
